The Holy Rosary

Weekly Mystery Guide

The Mysteries of the Rosary are assigned to specific days of the week to structure our devotions:

  • Monday & Saturday: The Joyful Mysteries
  • Tuesday & Friday: The Sorrowful Mysteries
  • Wednesday & Sunday: The Glorious Mysteries
  • Thursday: The Luminous Mysteries
The Joyful Mysteries
1. The Annunciation

The Angel Gabriel appears to Mary, announcing she will conceive the Son of God by the power of the Holy Spirit.

2. The Visitation

Mary journeys to visit her cousin Elizabeth, who recognizes her as the Mother of the Lord.

3. The Nativity

Jesus Christ is born in a humble stable in Bethlehem, wrapped in swaddling clothes and laid in a manger.

4. The Presentation

Mary and Joseph present the infant Jesus in the Temple according to the Law of Moses.

5. The Finding in the Temple

After three days of anxious searching, Mary and Joseph find the young Jesus teaching the doctors in the Temple.

The Luminous Mysteries
1. Baptism in the Jordan

The Holy Spirit descends upon Jesus as John baptizes Him, and the Father declares Him His beloved Son.

2. The Wedding at Cana

At Mary's request, Jesus performs His first public miracle, transforming water into wine.

3. Proclamation of the Kingdom

Jesus calls all to repentance and proclaims the arrival of the Kingdom of God.

4. The Transfiguration

Jesus reveals His divine glory to Peter, James, and John atop Mount Tabor.

5. Institution of the Eucharist

At the Last Supper, Jesus offers His Body and Blood under the appearances of bread and wine.

The Sorrowful Mysteries
1. The Agony in the Garden

Jesus prays in anguish in the Garden of Gethsemane on the eve of His passion, submitting His will to the Father.

2. The Scourging at the Pillar

Jesus is tied to a stone pillar and brutally scourged by Roman soldiers.

3. The Crowning with Thorns

A crown of sharp thorns is forced onto the head of Jesus to mock His kingship.

4. The Carrying of the Cross

Jesus carries the heavy wooden cross through the streets of Jerusalem toward Calvary.

5. The Crucifixion
